# Decouple chapter finalization from context sync

Finalizing a chapter accepts and saves its text immediately, even when its changes have not yet been incorporated into the work's long-term context files. Such a chapter is marked context-pending and remains the authoritative input for a later Context Sync; a failed or interrupted sync does not turn it back into a draft. Noya attempts the sync immediately after finalization and immediately retries recoverable technical failures; if the process was interrupted, it retries as soon as the task is restored. Ambiguities or conflicts that require a creative decision are not retried blindly and instead return to the Context Agent to resolve with the author. A chapter remains pending only after automatic recovery cannot complete or while such a decision is outstanding, and Noya must bring preceding finalized chapters up to date before preparing a subsequent chapter for writing. This preserves the author's sense that accepted prose is already finished while preventing later writing from consuming stale character, world, outline, or foreshadowing material.
